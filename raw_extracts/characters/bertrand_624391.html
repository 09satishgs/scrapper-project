How to Recruit Bertrand

Complete Bertrand's Paralogue in Part 1

Route and Chapter Dates
Cai's Path Ch. 10
 9/17 - 9/25
Dietrich's Path Ch. 9
 9/17
Theodora's Path Ch. 11
 9/17 - 9/25

Bertrand cannot be recruited in Part 1, but his paralogue is available on multiple paths. Make sure to complete this before the deadline to guarantee his appearance in Part 3. Beware that this only lasts for one in-game day on Dietrich's Path and is unavailable on Leda's Path.

Bertrand's Paralogue Walkthrough

Automatically Joins in Part 3 First Section

If you were able to complete Bertrand's Paralogue in Part 1, he should join during the events of Part 3: Salvation during the First Section.
